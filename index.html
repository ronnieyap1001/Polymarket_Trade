What I do

Jodoo implementations that fix three recurring SME problems.

Outcomes, not module names. The build is shaped around what’s costing you visibility, accuracy, or institutional memory today.

01
Problem: Problems surface too late.

Real-time visibility of work, parts, and quality.

See the state of every job, every part, and every quality check as it happens — not at the end of the week when the spreadsheets reconcile. Issues escalate while they’re still small.

02
Problem: Data you can’t trust.

One source of truth, captured once, accurate.

The number on the report matches the number on the floor. Data is entered where the work happens, once, and flows through the system. No reconciling three spreadsheets to find out what’s real.

03
Problem: Know-how walks out the door.

Processes live in the system, not in people’s heads.

When the senior storeman, the QA lead, or the production planner leaves, the process stays. The system encodes how the work is done — new hires onboard against it instead of guessing.
